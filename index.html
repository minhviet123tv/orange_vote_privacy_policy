Privacy Policy

Updated: September 2026. Applies to the Quiz Game VN app on your device.

1. What we collect

Account: email / Google ID, login ID, or anonymous session. Display profile: name, avatar, public ID, areas of interest (if you choose). Content you create or submit: polls/quizzes, choices, votes, comments, photos/videos, rewards, star ratings, feedback. Minimal technical logs for operation (errors, push notifications if permitted). Location data is used only when you enable the "find nearby users" feature.

2. How we use the data

Operating Quiz Game VN: displaying the feed, search, friends/following, leaderboards, rewards, notifications related to content you follow, community protection (moderating sensitive content), and improving the user experience.

We do not sell personal data to third parties for advertising purposes.

3. Storage and Processors

Account data and content are stored on Firebase (Google). Photos/videos may be stored in object storage (Cloudflare R2). Photos from Pexels.com belong to Pexels; the app only retrieves the URL/image you select. Data transmission occurs via HTTPS.

4. Sharing

Your name, avatar, public polls/quizzes, comments, and reviews may be visible to other Quiz Game VN users. We may disclose data when required by law or to protect user safety.

5. Your Rights

You can edit your profile, delete content you created (within the app's limits), log out, or request account deletion via the "Support & Feedback" section. When you delete a poll or quiz, that content is removed from the app. Certain technical records or violation reports may be retained for a reasonable period to ensure safety and legal compliance (if applicable).

6. Content and Copyright

You must not repost videos (or video clips) from other platforms unless you are the owner or hold legal rights to do so. Violations may result in content removal, feature restrictions, or legal action regarding copyright. You are responsible for the photos you take or upload. Photos from Pexels are subject to the Pexels license. You can report violations via the three-dot menu on a poll.

7. Children

Quiz Game VN is not intended for children under 13 years of age (or the minimum age required by the laws of your location). If we discover an account belonging to a child, we may lock or delete it.

8. Contact

For questions regarding privacy, please use the "Support & Feedback" section in Settings. If the policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
