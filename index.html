Privacy policy

Updated: September 2026. Applies to the Quiz Game VN app on your device.

1. What we collect

Account: Google email / ID, sign-in ID, or an anonymous session. Display profile: name, photo, public ID, and interest areas (if you choose them). Content you create or send: polls / quizzes, options, ballots, comments, photos/videos, rewards, star ratings, and feedback. Minimal technical logs to run the app (errors, and push notifications if you allow them). Location is used only when you turn on nearby people search.

2. How we use data

To operate Quiz Game VN: show the feed, search, friends / following, leaderboards, rewards, notifications about content you follow, community safety (sensitive-content moderation), and product improvement. We do not sell personal data to third parties for advertising.

3. Storage and processors

Account data and content are stored on Firebase (Google). Photos/videos may be stored in object storage (Cloudflare R2). Photos from Pexels.com belong to Pexels; the app only uses the URL or image you select. Servers may be located outside Vietnam. Data is transmitted over HTTPS.

4. Sharing

Your name, profile photo, public polls / quizzes, comments, and ratings may be visible to other Quiz Game VN users. We may disclose data when required by law or to protect user safety.

5. Your rights

You can edit your profile, delete content you created (within what the app allows), sign out, or request account deletion through Support & feedback. When you delete a poll / quiz, that content is removed from the app. Some technical records or abuse reports may be kept for a reasonable period for safety and legal compliance (if needed).

6. Content and copyright

You must not re-upload videos (or clips) from other platforms unless you own them or have a legal right. Violations may lead to content removal, feature limits, or action under copyright law. You are responsible for photos you take or upload. Pexels photos follow the Pexels license. You can report a violation from a poll’s three-dot menu.

7. Children

Quiz Game VN is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required by law where you live). If we find a child’s account, we may lock or delete it.

8. Contact

Privacy questions: use Support & feedback in Settings. When this policy changes, we update the date at the top of this page.
